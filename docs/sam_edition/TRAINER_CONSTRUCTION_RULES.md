# Pokémon: Sam Edition — Trainer Construction Rules

Current explicit trainer-data authority. These rules govern ordinary trainer cleanup without reopening already-authored major battles or Gym scripts.

## Ordinary trainers
- Build a coherent moveset from moves the Pokémon can reasonably know by its current level.
- Do not grant TM or HM moves.
- Do not grant held items unless the Pokémon naturally carries that item under the current Sam species/encounter rules.
- A coherent level-up set may retain useful earlier level-up moves rather than blindly using the four most recent moves.

## Cooltrainers
- Follow the ordinary-trainer baseline, except a Cooltrainer may use HM moves that are reasonable for the player's current point in progression.
- Non-Gym Cooltrainers use intentionally over-stylized / “tragedeigh” spellings of recognizable human names.

## Gym trainers
- Preserve the existing Gym's authored trainer IDs, party bindings, scripts, flags, dialogue, and map choreography.
- A Gym trainer may use that Gym's awarded TM on a compatible Pokémon.
- Gym trainer display names are puns related to the Gym's current specialty.
- If a Gym trainer is also a Cooltrainer, the type-pun rule takes priority and may use a trendy spelling.

## Scientists
- Scientist Pokémon tend to hold evolution items where appropriate to the team concept. This is a tendency rather than a requirement that every Scientist Pokémon hold an item.

## Dialogue
- Preserve ordinary trainer dialogue by default.
- Rewrite ordinary trainer dialogue only when that trainer's overworld sprite/presentation was changed and the old dialogue no longer fits.
- Preserve already-authored Gym trainer dialogue; changing a display name alone does not reopen Gym dialogue.
- Major/story-character dialogue remains controlled by that character's specialist authority.

## Major authored battles
Rivals, Gym Leaders, Elite Four, Team Rocket bosses, Satoshi, Thomas, and other specifically authored story encounters are not normalized by these ordinary-trainer rules. Their specialist battle packages remain controlling.
